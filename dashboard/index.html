Scanning a Survivor's Phone

  1. Open a case on the Cases page and add the phone on the Devices page.
  2. Click Pair scanner next to the phone to get a one-time code.
  3. On the phone, enter the code in the Argus scanner app and tap Scan device.
  4. The scan appears on the Scans page within seconds. Results are never sent back to the phone.
