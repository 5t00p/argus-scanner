Scan Reports

Argus helps users understand potential stalkerware threats detected during scans. Reports summarize suspicious applications and provide information about security risks.

Report Information

  • Scan ID: Identifies a completed scan.
  • Device: Identifies the scanned device.
  • Findings: Lists suspicious applications.
  • Risk Level: Indicates potential threats.
  • Status: Shows the scan's current status.

Understanding Risk Levels

High Risk: Applications with potentially dangerous behavior or permissions.

Medium Risk: Applications that may require additional review.

Low Risk: Applications with fewer suspicious indicators.

Detection results are indicators, not definitive proof of stalkerware.
